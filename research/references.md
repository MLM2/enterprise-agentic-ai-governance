# Reference starting points

These are foundational sources to read and map to individual controls; inclusion does not imply the proposed design has been validated by them.

1. NIST, *AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)* (2023): https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1
2. NIST, *AI RMF Generative AI Profile* (2024): https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1
3. NIST, *Cybersecurity Framework 2.0* (2024): https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29
4. NIST, *Digital Identity Guidelines*, SP 800-63 series: https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/
5. OWASP, *Top 10 for LLM Applications*: https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/
6. MITRE, *ATLAS — Adversarial Threat Landscape for AI Systems*: https://atlas.mitre.org/

## Research practice
For each source, record publication/version date, applicable requirement, assumptions, and whether the relationship is direct guidance or the author's inference. Avoid presenting voluntary frameworks as binding regulation.
